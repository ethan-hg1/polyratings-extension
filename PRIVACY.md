# Privacy Policy — Polyratings for Cal Poly Portal

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- **What it does:** annotates instructor names on the Cal Poly class search
  portal with ratings from [Polyratings](https://polyratings.dev/).
- **Matching happens entirely on your device.** Instructor names visible on
  the portal page are compared, locally in your browser, against a cached
  list of Polyratings professors. Nothing about the page you're viewing, your
  identity, or your portal session is read, stored, or sent anywhere.
- **The only network request this extension makes** is an anonymous,
  unauthenticated fetch of the public professor list from
  `api-prod.polyratings.org`, refreshed roughly every 24 hours and cached
  locally in your browser's extension storage. That request contains no
  identifying information — it's the same public data anyone can fetch from
  Polyratings' API.
- **No analytics, telemetry, or third-party trackers** are included.
- **No data is sold or shared** — there's no server component collecting
  anything, so there's nothing to share.

Questions? Open an issue on the
[GitHub repo](https://github.com/ethan-hg1/polyratings-extension).
